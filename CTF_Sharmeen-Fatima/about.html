Our Story

Turning aspirations into addresses

Dream House began with a simple frustration: house-hunting in Karachi meant chasing brokers who oversold and undersold in the same breath. We set out to do the opposite — show fewer houses, but show the right ones.

Today we work across DHA, Clifton, Bahria Town and a handful of neighbourhoods we know well enough to describe honestly — traffic at rush hour, school runs, and all.
